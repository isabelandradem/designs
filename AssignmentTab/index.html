Assignments Tab: Separate Lessons from Assessments

Jira Ticket: KKIDS-8474

Current experience

The Assignments tab currently shows all assigned activities in one list, grouped by date. This includes both lessons and assessments.

Right now, the only way to tell them apart is the label on the activity thumbnail: Main / Practice / Basic for lessons, and Assessment for assessments (see KKIDS-8476).

Why this matters

Assessments need to happen in a controlled setting to produce reliable data. If a child completes an assessment at home, they may not have teacher support, or they may get help from a parent. This can make the results less valid.
